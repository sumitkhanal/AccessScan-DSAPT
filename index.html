AccessScan

DSAPT Accessibility Audit Tool · v1.0

About

AccessScan uses computer vision (YOLOv8) and monocular depth estimation (MiDaS) to automatically audit public transport stops against the Disability Standards for Accessible Public Transport (DSAPT), in partnership with La Trobe University and DITRDCA.
